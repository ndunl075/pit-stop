# Original artwork and audio

The car and crew SVGs, pit-bay drawing code, animation keyframes, icons, and synthesized sound cues were created for Pit Stop and are licensed under the project's MIT license. They depict fictional vehicles and a fictional team, without licensed racing marks or third-party designs. No fonts, remote artwork, or recorded audio are bundled. The interface uses system fonts. PixiJS, React, Vite and Tauri are separately licensed dependencies; inspect the lockfile and dependency license audit before distributing a binary.
